What the summary would say

In the synthetic dataset, month 3 sales are higher than month 2 while order count is unchanged. The arithmetic points to a higher average order value; it does not explain why customers spent more. Product mix, pricing, and refund timing would need review before recommending action.

Metric glossary and validation

  • Net sales: completed merchandise revenue less discounts and refunds; excludes shipping and taxes in this example.
  • Orders: distinct completed, non-test order IDs in the reporting period.
  • Average order value: net sales divided by orders, calculated consistently for each period.
  • Checks: unique order IDs, reconciled totals, documented refund treatment, complete reporting dates, and an agreed time zone.

Operational handoff

A project would provide the dashboard or reporting model, source map, validation notes, and refresh instructions. The client-designated owner operates refreshes, permissions, and maintenance after handoff. This example is a monthly report, not a live incident-monitoring dashboard.
