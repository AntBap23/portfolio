Consulting/Process
Process & project fit

Know what happens before the work starts.

Projects and fractional partnerships are scoped upfront, completed outside my full-time role, and managed through written updates, planned reviews, clear capacity, and usable deliverables.

Working model

Async-first, not hands-off.

Project work moves between scheduled meetings. Fractional clients reserve an average of 8–12 hours a week with agreed priorities and two review calls a month. Up to 15 hours can be scheduled for planned push weeks—without creating daytime or on-call coverage.

Fit check

A focused model for teams with a real decision to support.

The strongest engagements have a clear owner, useful data access, and enough stakeholder availability to define the problem and review the work.

Good fit

Bring me in when…

  • You have one important product, customer, funnel, or KPI decision—or a recurring queue of them.
  • Useful data exists in exports, spreadsheets, or an existing analytics platform.
  • A small team needs analysis without hiring a full-time analyst.
  • Async progress and scheduled reviews work for your team.
  • You can provide a decision owner and timely access to the relevant data.

Probably not a fit

Another model may be better when…

  • You need analytics implementation, production engineering, or tag deployment.
  • The work requires access to an employer's confidential methods or data.
  • The project overlaps with my employer, its direct competitors, or a conflict-of-interest area.
  • The project requires daily operations, urgent production support, or frequent business-hours meetings.
  • There is no clear decision owner or usable data source.
Engagement rhythm

Focused projects, clear milestones, useful handoff.

Your team knows what I need, what I am reviewing or building, when feedback matters, and what happens after delivery.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Clarify the decision, stakeholders, current workflow, data sources, constraints, and success criteria.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Review data quality, definitions, setup, reporting gaps, and the smallest useful scope.

  3. 03

    Build & analyze

    Create the dashboard, readout, reporting model, forecast, or AI workflow with a written midpoint update.

  4. 04

    Deliver & enable

    Walk through the output, refine it, document the work, and define ownership or next steps.

Project cadence: discovery, kickoff, async work, midpoint update, and handoff. Fractional cadence: monthly priorities, written weekly updates, two review calls, and an average of 8–12 hours per week, with planned capacity up to 15 hours when needed.

What I need from your team

Enough access to understand the problem—and enough feedback to keep the work useful.

01

A decision owner

One person who can clarify the business question, prioritize tradeoffs, and confirm what success looks like.

02

Relevant access

Appropriate exports, dashboards, definitions, documentation, or least-privilege tool access. Any required paid software seat remains client-provided.

03

Milestone feedback

Written input and scheduled reviews at agreed points so the output reflects how stakeholders will use it.

04

A handoff owner

Someone who can maintain, use, or sponsor the deliverable after the project closes.

FAQ

What teams usually want to know before starting.

For scope-specific questions, email the project details and I will tell you whether it fits.

Ask a question
What do you help consulting clients with?

I help teams clean up messy reporting, define KPIs, build dashboards, analyze digital behavior, evaluate experiments, and create a repeatable analytics decision rhythm.

Who is a good fit?

The best projects have a clear business owner, a real decision to support, useful data access, and stakeholders who can provide feedback at agreed milestones.

How do projects begin?

We begin with a discovery conversation and kickoff that clarify the decision, current workflow, data sources, stakeholders, scope, and success criteria.

Can we work together asynchronously?

Yes. Projects use scoped deliverables, written updates, and scheduled milestone reviews. Your team needs to provide data access, feedback, and decision-maker input at agreed points.

Who provides the software and platform access?

Your company provides any required paid platform access through a named, least-privilege account. I can work from secure exports with Excel, SQL, or Python and will not publish confidential data to Tableau Public or another public portfolio platform.

Can we hire you as a fractional partner?

Yes. Fractional work averages 8–12 hours per week with a three-month minimum, agreed monthly priorities, written updates, and two scheduled review calls. Up to 15 hours can be reserved during planned reporting, launch, or experimentation cycles. It is not embedded 9–5 or on-call coverage.

Do you provide same-day analytics support?

No. Consulting is scheduled around a full-time role and built around defined deliverables, capped capacity, written updates, and planned reviews rather than on-call support.
