A practical working relationship.

Bring a decision owner, relevant data, milestone feedback, and someone who can take ownership at handoff. This model suits startups, founders, agencies, small businesses, and growing teams that value structured project delivery.

Every engagement has an agreed scope, deliverables, milestones, deadlines, and review points before work begins. Additional work, revisions beyond the agreed scope, maintenance, and support require separate scoping and approval. Services do not include emergency response, on-call coverage, real-time monitoring, or live experimentation operations.

Common questions.

Can I inquire without booking a call?

Yes. Send your business challenge and desired deliverables through the project inquiry form or email. A meeting is optional; scope, timeline, deliverables, and price are proposed before work begins.

When are live sessions available?

Consultations are available by appointment, with flexible evening and weekend scheduling. All live sessions take place at or after 5:00 p.m. Central Time on weekdays or during mutually agreed weekend hours. Email communication can occur throughout the day; immediate responses are not guaranteed.

What happens after technical handoff?

Your designated team owns dashboard operation and AI workflow maintenance after handoff. Documentation explains validation, limitations, and ownership. Additional maintenance or support must be separately scoped and approved.

Do you run live A/B tests?

No. The Digital Experience & Growth Opportunity Audit delivers an assessment, evidence-based recommendations, and a prioritized backlog. Your team may later validate recommendations; live experiment operations and guaranteed conversion or revenue gains are not included.

How does recurring support work?

Monthly Reporting Support delivers agreed KPI summaries, analysis, dashboard updates, insights, and recommendations. Structured Analytics Advisory provides scheduled strategy consultations and periodic deliverables. Both use defined monthly scopes and capped capacity.

Who provides tools and data?

Clients provide relevant secure exports or least-privilege access and any required paid platform licenses. Work can use Excel, SQL, Python, or existing BI tools; confidential data is not published to public portfolio platforms.
