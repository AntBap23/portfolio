My approach

Start with the question. Build only what helps answer it.

My work begins with the business question and the person who needs to act on the answer. Then I define the measurement, inspect the data, build the simplest useful output, and explain the result in language stakeholders can use.

My full-time role is the center of my professional development. Outside of it, I take on a limited number of focused projects and capped fractional partnerships. I work best with teams that value clear ownership, async progress, realistic capacity, and honest recommendations about what the data can and cannot support.

Tools matter, but they do not lead the conversation. A dashboard, experiment readout, or reporting model is useful only when the team understands the metric, trusts the input, and knows what decision the output should inform.
