Questions before you start.

What is included in a project?

Every engagement has an agreed scope, deliverables, milestones, deadlines, and review points before work begins. Each service card above lists its expected deliverables.

What is not included?

Services do not include emergency response, on-call coverage, real-time monitoring, or live experimentation operations. Additional work, revisions beyond the agreed scope, maintenance, and support require separate scoping and approval.

Do I need to buy a BI tool?

Not necessarily. Excel, SQL, Python, or an existing BI platform can be used where appropriate. Your team supplies relevant secure exports or least-privilege access and any required paid software licenses.

Should I start with an audit or a sprint?

An analytics audit identifies the decision, metric gaps, data quality issues, and reporting workflow problems before a team invests in a build. A Dashboard & Reporting Sprint builds the reporting deliverable itself, with KPI definitions, validation checks, documentation, and handoff. You can start with an analytics or digital experience audit, build a reporting or AI workflow deliverable, or agree a recurring reporting or advisory package.

How is the final price set?

Published ranges are in USD and depend on the agreed deliverables, data readiness, and complexity. The Digital Experience & Growth Opportunity Audit and the Applied AI Workflow Sprint are quoted after scoping. Scope, timeline, and price are confirmed in a written proposal before any work begins.

Who are these services for?

These services cover analytics, business intelligence, digital experience assessment, and applied AI for startups, founders, Chicago and other small businesses, agencies, nonprofits, workforce boards, and growing organizations.
