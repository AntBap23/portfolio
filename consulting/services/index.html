Product Analytics Decision Sprint

$750–$1,250

Best for: Small SaaS, membership, marketplace, nonprofit, or service teams with customer or product data but no dedicated product analyst.

What you bring

  • One clear business decision
  • A decision owner for kickoff and feedback
  • Relevant exports or least-privilege tool access
  • Existing KPI, funnel, campaign, or customer data

What you receive

  • KPI and measurement map
  • Funnel, segment, trend, or behavior analysis
  • Decision-ready dashboard or analysis pack
  • Three to five prioritized findings
  • 90-day measurement and experiment roadmap
  • Final walkthrough and handoff notes

Tools and access: Your company provides any paid platform access required for the work. I can analyze exports with Excel, SQL, or Python and deliver in a practical format without requiring you to buy a new BI tool. Confidential data is never published to a public portfolio platform.

Not included: analytics implementation, production data engineering, tag deployment, Adobe Target configuration, or ongoing product management.

Dashboard & Measurement Build

$1,500–$3,500

Best for: Teams that have outgrown ad hoc spreadsheets or conflicting dashboards and need a repeatable view of performance.

What we can build

  • KPI definitions and ownership map
  • Funnel, cohort, customer, or operating scorecard
  • Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, or analysis-pack workflow
  • QA checks and reporting documentation

What you receive

  • Reusable dashboard or reporting model
  • Metric glossary and source map
  • Decision-focused views and filters
  • Handoff walkthrough and maintenance notes

Tools and access: I can build inside your licensed environment or work from secure exports. If your team does not own a BI platform, we can scope the work around Excel, SQL, Python, or another practical no-new-license option.

Not included: production data pipelines, enterprise architecture, ongoing administration, or unrestricted business-hours support.
