FAQ

How to read these projects

What's the difference between an analytics audit and a dashboard build?

An analytics audit identifies the decision, metric gaps, data quality issues, and reporting workflow problems before a team invests in a build. A dashboard build creates the reporting asset itself, including cleaned data, visual views, KPI definitions, QA checks, and handoff notes.

How does experimentation experience inform consulting?

My experimentation background informs evidence-based assessments of customer journeys, measurement, and digital experience opportunities. The portfolio includes A/B analysis; independent services focus on scoped assessments and recommendations rather than live test operations.
