# Client Sourcing Playbook

Updated: September 21, 2026

## Positioning to use

Anthony Baptiste is a Digital Experimentation Analyst at Ulta Beauty and an independent product analytics consultant. He supports a small number of clients outside his full-time role through defined projects and capped fractional partnerships.

Ways to work together:

- Product Analytics Decision Sprint: 10 business days for one high-value question.
- Dashboard & Measurement Build: 3–5 weeks for a reusable reporting foundation.
- Fractional Product Analytics Partner: 8–12 hours per week on average, three-month minimum, for recurring analysis and decision support. Up to 15 hours may be planned for heavier reporting, launch, or experimentation cycles.

This is consulting, not a second 9–5: async-first, scheduled, capped, and outcome-based.

## Prospect filters

Prioritize:

- Small SaaS, marketplace, membership, nonprofit, or digital-service teams
- Roughly 5-75 employees or an obviously founder-led product team
- A live product with an observable activation, engagement, conversion, or retention journey
- A recent launch, feature release, early-access program, new market, or visible customer-feedback loop
- A founder, product lead, growth lead, customer-success lead, or operations leader who can own a decision
- Chicago and the Midwest first, then remote US companies

Exclude:

- Beauty, cosmetics, or direct retail competitors of Ulta Beauty
- Ulta vendors, partners, or companies where a conflict could reasonably exist
- Analytics agencies and BI consultancies as direct clients; treat strong agencies as possible referral or subcontract partners instead
- Companies that primarily need tracking implementation, production data engineering, or full-time support
- Any prospect that would require employer-confidential knowledge, methods, data, or work product

Search separately for two opportunity types:

1. **Direct clients:** founder, product, growth, customer-success, or operations leaders who own the decision.
2. **Channel partners:** product studios, growth agencies, fractional executives, accelerators, and independent consultants who can refer or subcontract analytics work.

## Master sourcing prompt

```text
You are a careful B2B prospect researcher helping an independent product analytics consultant.

Today is September 19, 2026.

POSITIONING
Anthony Baptiste is a Digital Experimentation Analyst at Ulta Beauty and an independent product analytics consultant. Outside his full-time role, he offers three async-first options:
1. Product Analytics Decision Sprint: 10 business days, $750-$1,250.
2. Dashboard & Measurement Build: 3-5 weeks, $1,500-$3,500.
3. Fractional Product Analytics Partner: 8-12 hours per week on average, three-month minimum, $2,000-$4,000 per month. Up to 15 hours may be scheduled during planned push weeks; heavier scopes are generally $4,000-$5,000 per month.

He does not provide embedded 9-5 coverage, same-day support, production data engineering, tag deployment, or unrestricted staff augmentation.

IDEAL PROSPECT
- US-based small SaaS, marketplace, membership, nonprofit, or digital-service organization
- Prefer Chicago and the Midwest, but remote US companies are acceptable
- Approximately 5-75 employees or clearly founder-led
- Has a live product and observable activation, engagement, conversion, retention, or customer journey
- Has a recent public signal: launch, feature release, early access, expansion, customer-feedback request, hiring, funding, partnership, or product update
- Likely has useful data but may not have a senior product analytics function or enough work for a full-time analytics hire
- Can work asynchronously and provide exports or least-privilege access

EXCLUDE
- Beauty, cosmetics, and direct retail competitors of Ulta Beauty
- Ulta partners or vendors when a conflict may exist
- Analytics agencies, consultancies, staffing firms, and BI implementation shops as direct-client prospects; allow strong product or growth agencies only in the separate referral-partner table
- Public companies and large enterprises
- Companies whose primary need is production engineering, tracking implementation, or full-time support
- Any prospect that would require sensitive personal data for initial qualification

TASK
Find 30 direct-client prospects and 10 channel-partner opportunities using current public web sources. Verify that each company appears active as of 2026. Do not invent names, roles, emails, company size, funding, or activity. If a field cannot be verified, write "Unverified." Never provide guessed email addresses.

For every prospect return:
1. Company
2. Website
3. City/state
4. Product and business model
5. Most recent public activity signal and date
6. The specific product analytics question Anthony could help answer
7. Best target role
8. Public name of the likely decision-maker, only if verified
9. Public LinkedIn/profile/contact-page URL
10. A personalized opening sentence based on the cited signal
11. Best engagement fit: Decision Sprint, Measurement Build, Fractional Partner, or Referral Partner
12. Why that engagement fits
13. Fit score from 1-10
14. Conflict-risk flag: low, review, or exclude
15. Two supporting source URLs

Sort direct clients and channel partners into separate tables, then sort by fit score and recency. End with the five direct prospects and three partner opportunities Anthony should research first and explain why.
```

## Human review before outreach

Before emailing any prospect:

1. Recheck the company site and recent activity.
2. Confirm the person still holds the target role.
3. Confirm the company is not a conflict with Ulta Beauty or its policies.
4. Remove any claim that depends on private or inferred information.
5. Personalize the opening line around one verifiable product signal.
6. Recommend one specific engagement—sprint, measurement build, or fractional partnership—based on the evidence.
7. Send only from a professional account you control and track opt-outs.
